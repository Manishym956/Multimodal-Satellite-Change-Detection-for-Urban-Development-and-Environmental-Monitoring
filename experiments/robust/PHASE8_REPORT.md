## 3. RQ2 (primary): does B's F1 change with the age of the historical image?

- **V:** mean slope +0.0083 F1 per year, 95% CI [-0.0021, +0.0187], t p 0.105: **no detectable effect of the gap**.
- The pre-registered test is the t-test. The exact Wilcoxon test on the same 10 slopes gives p 0.049: the two
  disagree at α = 0.05, so the slope's sign is suggestive (positive in 8/10 seeds), not established.
- Test F1 of B by tag (mean ± SD): OSCD date 1 0.420 ± 0.039; 1 month 0.389 ± 0.044; 3 months 0.422 ± 0.038; 6 months 0.409 ± 0.035;
  12 months 0.417 ± 0.037; 18 months 0.412 ± 0.033.
- **Sensitivity (all 10 cities):** mean slope +0.0042 F1 per year, 95% CI [-0.0002, +0.0086], t p 0.057: **no detectable effect of the gap**.
- **H:** under this design, the negative slope of Song et al. (2026: F1 83.99% at 1 month to 78.31% at 12 months) is
  not reproduced; if anything, the youngest image scores lowest. This is consistent with the pre-specified confound: an
  image 1 month before date 2 lies after date 1 and may already show part of the labelled change, which the pre-change
  slot would then hide. How much change each image shows was not measured, so the design cannot separate the image's age
  from that effect.

## 4. RQ1 by gap (secondary): B and the asynchronous model against SAR alone

- **V (B, Holm over 5):** no detectable difference at any age (mean Δ from -0.017 to +0.016, smallest Holm p 1.00). SAR-only U-Net F1 0.406 ± 0.048.
- **V (async, Holm over 5):** async lower at 1 month (Δ -0.058, 95% CI [-0.093, -0.022], Holm p 0.027); no detectable difference at 3, 6, 12 and 18 months.
- **Async slope (secondary):** mean slope +0.0161 F1 per year, 95% CI [-0.0001, +0.0322], t p 0.051: **no detectable effect of the gap**.
- **Source control (own family):** Earth Engine date-1 image − OSCD's own date-1 image, for B: Δ +0.000, 95% CI [-0.000, +0.001], Holm p 0.69
  (no detectable difference); B with OSCD's date-1 image − SAR-only: Δ +0.014, 95% CI [-0.017, +0.044], Holm p 0.69 (no detectable difference). **H:** the processing chain is
  not a confound, and Step 6's RQ1 null result is reproduced on the primary cities.
- **H:** at no age does a historical optical image give a detectable gain over bitemporal SAR, which extends Step 6's
  RQ1 result from OSCD's own date-1 image to images 1–18 months old.

## 5. RQ5 (secondary): false-alarm rate by WorldCover class, B@oscd − SAR-only

- **V:** B lower in shrubland (Δ -0.006, 95% CI [-0.010, -0.003], Holm p 0.013); no detectable difference in the other 6 classes (built-up: Δ -0.004, 95% CI [-0.015, +0.008], Holm p 0.98).
- Pooled pixel share of the classes tested: tree cover 15.6%; shrubland 1.3%; grassland 7.7%; cropland 8.0%; built-up 40.6%; bare 24.6%; water 1.7%.
- **H:** the historical optical image does not change false alarms in built-up areas (the largest class) by a detectable
  amount; the one detectable reduction is in a class covering about 1.3% of pixels and should not be generalised.
  WorldCover (2020) post-dates OSCD date 2, so classes are a stratification, not a label.

## 6. Limitations

- One benchmark (OSCD; 9 primary test cities), frozen models trained with OSCD's own date-1 image; no model was
  trained on older images. Seeds are training replicates on the same cities, not independent geography.
- The gap confounds the image's age with how much of the labelled change it already shows (section 3).
- Minimum detectable effect 1.00 SD of the paired slopes at α = 0.05 (1.33 SD at α = 0.05/5): a small effect of the gap
  cannot be excluded.
