## 5. Threshold-selection and test-evaluation audit

- Thresholds and checkpoints come only from validation cities (training cities under `hafner`, as in the
  official code). The code path is `train_robust.py`: `evaluate(model, train_ds, val_cities)` → `best_threshold`.
  The test set is evaluated once, after training (`evaluate(model, test_ds, test_cities)`).
- Every test-set evaluation made in this study:
  1. once at the end of each training run (every run in this report, plus a 20-step smoke run that was deleted);
  2. once per run by `reeval_robust.py` (a reproduction check; no selection). The first, stopped version
     evaluated `hafner_full_seed1` once more;
  3. once per `hafner_full` checkpoint by `gap_analysis_robust.py` (adds F1 at 0.66; no selection);
  4. the test F1 of the step sweep was **viewed** before the step budget was fixed. See §4: the decision was redone on validation.
- No test number was used to pick a checkpoint, a threshold, the loss, the augmentation or the Phase 2 arms.
- Phase 2: `PHASE2_PREREGISTRATION.md` was last modified 14:18:28 on 2026-10-21. The first Phase 2 run snapshot is from
  14:18:34 the same day. File times are the only evidence of the ordering. All 20 runs match the pre-registered
  arguments, seeds, split and frozen `train_robust.py` hash (`audit_p2.py`). Analyses added after the
  pre-registration, and labelled as such: the paired tests at 0.5, the Holm-adjusted Wilcoxon, 95% CIs and d_z.
- Seeds 1–3 of `p2_full` are the same configuration as `holdout_full_s2500`, whose test F1 had been seen while
  the step budget was being chosen. They were retrained from scratch and reproduce identically. No Phase 2 decision used them.

Pre-registered decision rule (Holm-adjusted paired t p < 0.05 on F1 at the validation-selected threshold): <!-- P2_INTERPRETATION -->

Reading of these results. Every statement is about OSCD, this network, these two arms and 10 seeds:

- **Supported (V):** under the pre-registered rule, dropout training (B) beats full-only training (A) on zero-filled
  post-change optical (`no_o2`) and on the copy-forward imputation (`o2_copy`). B > A in 10/10 seeds for both, and the Wilcoxon
  test agrees after Holm correction. `o2_copy` was never trained by either arm, so this
  is a transfer effect, not one learned from that condition.
- **Not supported (H):** an effect on all optical missing (`no_opt`). The mean difference is positive, but it comes mostly
  from one A seed (3) that collapsed under `no_opt` (Δ = +0.342 there; mean Δ over the other 9 seeds = +0.010).
  Seed by seed, the arms split 5/5. What B shows descriptively is
  a lower across-seed SD under `no_opt`; no test of variance was pre-registered.
- **Full-input cost:** at the validation-selected threshold, there is no detectable cost. Δ ≈ 0, the 95% CI of Δ is about
  ±0.035 F1, and the seeds split 5/5. A difference smaller than about 0.035 F1 cannot be excluded.
  At the fixed 0.5 threshold, B is lower on `full` (secondary, descriptive analysis). B's probabilities are shifted
  upward (higher recall, lower precision at 0.5; mean selected τ is higher for B). So the dropout model **needs
  threshold calibration on validation data**. A user who deploys at 0.5 would see a full-input cost.
  "Calibration" here means choosing the decision threshold on validation cities. Probability calibration
  (reliability curves, ECE) was not measured.
- **Mechanism (H):** A's validation-selected `no_o2` threshold is 0.67–0.99 across seeds (8/10 ≥ 0.90); B's is 0.54–0.87.
  This is consistent with the full-only late-fusion model reading a zero-filled post image as widespread change. B learns that zero-filled optical is uninformative. This explanation was not tested directly.
- B's validation score peaks earlier (selected steps are lower). Its selection score averages three conditions,
  as pre-registered, while A's uses `full` only. So the checkpoint rule differs between arms by design.
- Zero-filled input is **missing** imagery. None of this shows robustness to clouds, haze or partial occlusion.

## 7. Limitations and unresolved items

- OSCD has only 24 cities. The 4 validation and 10 test cities make threshold and checkpoint selection noisy, as the per-seed spread shows.
- Zero-filling simulates **missing** imagery. It is not a model of cloud-contaminated imagery. Cloud corruption and temporal gaps are untested here (later phases).
- `o2_copy` is an inference-time imputation. Neither arm trains on it.
- The DS-UNet 0.600 comparison is unresolved (§3).
- Two small code differences from the official training loop remain (batch sampling, evaluation orbit).
- The augmentation is a re-implementation of the official rules, not the official code.
- Phase 2 has 10 seeds on one dataset: 10 test cities, pooled pixel counts. Seeds capture training randomness, not
  city-level sampling variation, so the p-values do not support generalisation to other regions or sensors.
- One dropout mix was tested (0.5 / 0.25 / 0.25), not tuned. Whether other mixes avoid the threshold shift on full input is open.
- No comparison yet against Hafner & Ban (2023), the SAR→optical feature-reconstruction method for missing optical in urban mapping.
