# Step 5: recall-biased real-detector cloud masks on composite clouds (evaluation only)

Protocol: `PHASE5_PREREGISTRATION.md`, committed in `d9e29ff` (sections 1–11) and `5f5be9c` (§12: validation operating points,
smoke test, test detector run), before any change model was evaluated on a test composite. The tables in section 9 are
generated by the registered `analyze_phase5.py` (`phase5/tables.md`) and pasted here unchanged. **V** marks a result under the
pre-registered rule; **H** marks an interpretation. Every number in this report is filled in from `phase5/summary.json` by

**Scope.** Every result here is on **composites**: real cloud pixels from the Sentinel-2 Cloud Mask Catalogue pasted into
the OSCD post-change images. They are not real cloudy acquisitions (section 7). The detector is real (s2cloudless), and its
errors are real errors on real cloud pixels, but nothing here measures performance on actual cloudy satellite imagery.

## 3. Decision under the pre-specified guidance (§8 of the pre-registration)

The first branch requires rb95 better than bal at 30% **and** mean retained gain ≥ 0.75. rb95 is better than bal at 30%, but
B's mean retained gain for rb95 at c30 is **{RG_B_c30_rb95}**, below 0.75. The third branch requires rb95 worse than bal, or
retained gain < 0.5; neither holds. **No pre-specified branch applies exactly.** The result lies between the first and third
branches: a recall-biased real detector with zero-fill helps B under these composites and is better than the
accuracy-optimal mask at 30% and 60% cloud, but it retains about three quarters of the oracle gain at 30% (and
{RG_B_c60_rb95} at 60%, {RG_B_c10_rb95} at 10%). The value 0.740 is not rounded up to the threshold, and the threshold is not
revisited.

## 6. Interpretation (H)

- **Recall bias helps, partly by discarding the optical image.** rb95 beats bal at 30% and 60% cloud, but its masks cover more
  than half of the clear image, and the near-total mask rb99 does as well or better at c30 and c60. For the dropout-trained
  model, discarding most of a cloudy post-change image is cheaper than leaving cloud pixels in it. The gain over bal is
  therefore not evidence that the detector's recall-biased mask localises cloud well; it is consistent with B falling back to
  SAR and the pre-change image.
- **The real detector loses recall on test.** The validation operating point misses {DT_MISS_RANGE} of test cloud pixels at 10% and 30%
  cloud. This is the failure Steps 4A-bis and 5S identified as expensive (cloud-valued pixels reaching the model), and
  dilation cannot reach clouds the detector does not detect.
- **The remaining gap to the oracle is real.** rb95 is {ORACLE_GAP_RANGE} F1 below the oracle mask, and costs {CLEAN_rb95}
  F1 on clear images. Over-masking is not free here: Step 4A-bis found 2–8 px dilation nearly free (≤ 0.010 F1 at 30%), but
  the real rb95 mask covers more than half of the clear image, and B's F1 outside the cloud falls from {OUT_oracle_c30}
  (oracle) to {OUT_rb95_c30} (rb95) at c30.
- **Modality-dropout training remains necessary.** For the full-only model A, zero-fill under rb95 is worse than no fill at
  10% and 30% cloud and indistinguishable at 60%; recall-biased masking is useful only for a model trained to work without
  the optical image.
- **Next step (pre-specified options, each to be pre-registered separately):** the third-branch remedies remain the candidates
  for closing the oracle gap: a validity-mask input channel, or training with cloud-valued corruption. A test on real cloudy
  acquisitions is needed before any claim of robustness to real clouds.

## 7. Limitations

- **Composites, not real acquisitions.** Cloud pixels are real, but they are pasted from other scenes: thin cloud carries the
  foreign surface, there are no matching shadows, the cloud edge is a hard boundary between two scenes, and the atmosphere outside
  the cloud is unchanged. Only the post-change date is clouded. **These results are not performance on cloudy satellite
  acquisitions.**
- **Detector false detections on clean images** are on OSCD surfaces and may include real, unlabelled cloud.
- **Labels are the catalogue's semi-automatic 20 m masks,** upsampled; boundary errors in M count as detector errors.
- **One detector** (s2cloudless at 10 m on 10 bands), one zero-fill strategy, one architecture.
- **Operating points at the grid boundary:** rb99 did not reach its target and sits at the grid edge (flagged, §12 of the
  pre-registration).
- **Arm C may be advantaged** on composites from bank scenes ({N_BANK_TEST} of 90 test composites).
- **Seeds are not geography.** The 10 seeds are training replicates evaluated on the same 10 official OSCD test cities.
- **Resumed composites:** 89 of 126 composite files were kept from interrupted build runs and verified by mask, shape, dtype
  and value range, not byte for byte (§11 of the pre-registration).
- Zero is the fill value used here, not an intrinsic missing-data token.
