## 3. RQ3 (primary): SAR-only U-Net, Holm over 2

- **V:** adding VH lowers F1: **{L_VVVH}** ({D_VVVH}); replacing VV by VH lowers it further: **{L_VH}** ({D_VH}).
- Test F1 on {N_CITIES} cities (mean ± SD): VV {F_U_VV}; VH {F_U_VH}; VV+VH {F_U_VVVH}.
- **H:** under this protocol, RQ3's hypothesis (an additional polarization improves SAR change detection) is not
  supported for SAR alone: VV alone scores best and VH alone worst. This does not test Song et al.'s physical argument
  for VH on construction-ready bare land; it shows that on OSCD urban change, with 3-month mean backscatter and this
  recipe, adding VH to VV lowers F1.

## 4. Secondary results

- **DS-UNet, full input (own Holm family):** VV+VH − VV {D_DS_VVVH} ({L_DS_VVVH}); VH − VV {D_DS_VH} ({L_DS_VH}).
  F1: VV {F_D_VV}; VH {F_D_VH}; VV+VH {F_D_VVVH}. **H:** once optical data are present, polarization does not
  measurably matter.
- **False-alarm rate by WorldCover class, SAR-only U-Net, VV+VH − VV (Holm over {N_CLASSES}):** VV+VH lower in
  {FAR_LOWER}; no detectable difference in {FAR_NONE}; higher in none.
- **H (trade-off, not discrimination):** the fewer false alarms come with fewer detections. VV+VH's recall is lower than
  VV's in every class tested and overall ({R_VVVH} vs {R_VV}; precision {P_VVVH} vs {P_VV}), so the model flags less
  change everywhere, and its F1 is lower. This is not evidence that VH separates urban development from environmental
  pseudo-change.
- **Descriptive (10 cities, Las Vegas included):** SAR-only U-Net VV+VH F1 falls to {F10_U_VVVH} (false-alarm rate
  {FAR10_U_VVVH}) against {F10_U_VV} for VV. One city with an all-zero VH band is enough to nearly halve the pooled F1 of the
  model trained with VV+VH, so a missing polarization is itself a robustness risk.

## 5. Limitations

- One benchmark (OSCD; {N_CITIES} test cities), one recipe, 3-month mean backscatter, VH range fixed at [−30, 0] dB;
  seeds are training replicates on the same cities, not independent geography.
- Thresholds are each run's validation-selected full-input threshold; the false-alarm and recall shifts depend on them.
- Minimum detectable effect 1.14 SD of the paired differences at α = 0.05/2.
