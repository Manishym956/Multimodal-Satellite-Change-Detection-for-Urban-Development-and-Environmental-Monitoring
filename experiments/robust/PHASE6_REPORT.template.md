## 3. RQ1 (primary): SAR + historical optical vs SAR alone, Holm over 2

{RQ1_TABLE}

- **V:** **no detectable difference** for B with the historical optical image ({D_B_SAR}) and for the asynchronous
  model trained only on SAR + historical optical ({D_ASYNC_SAR}).
- **Secondary:** async − B at `no_o2` ({D_ASYNC_B}); within B, with the historical image minus without it
  ({D_B_NOOPT}). Neither is detectable.
- **Descriptive:** the asynchronous model, trained only with the historical optical image, scores {ASYNC_NOOPT} when that
  image is also removed, against {ASYNC_NOO2} with it.
- **H:** under a shared protocol, the historical (pre-change) optical image gives no measurable gain over bitemporal SAR
  for urban change on OSCD. The companion paper's earlier margin (missing-optical 0.309 vs SAR-only 0.241, different
  protocol) does not survive: the SAR-only U-Net reaches {F_SAR} under this protocol. The minimum detectable effect is
  about 1.14 SD of the paired differences (Holm over 2), so a small benefit cannot be excluded.

## 5. Limitations

- One benchmark (OSCD; 10 test cities), VV only, one recipe; seeds are training replicates on the same test cities.
- Each U-Net baseline is the official DS-UNet U-Net, trained with the dual-stream recipe; other baseline designs or
  tuning budgets could score differently.
- The historical optical image here is OSCD's own date-1 image; the effect of its age is Step 8 (RQ2).
