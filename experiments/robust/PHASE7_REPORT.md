## 2. Data

- Registered city set: the 9 test cities with VH at both dates (brasilia, montpellier, norcia, rio, saclay_w, valencia, dubai, milano, chongqing). Las Vegas is excluded because its
  evaluated orbit has no VH scene at date 1 (an all-zero band); it enters the 10-city descriptive table only.

## 3. RQ3 (primary): SAR-only U-Net, Holm over 2

- **V:** adding VH lowers F1: **VV+VH lower** (Δ -0.044, 95% CI [-0.065, -0.023], Holm p 0.0011); replacing VV by VH lowers it further: **VH lower** (Δ -0.070, 95% CI [-0.091, -0.048], Holm p 8.8e-05).
- Test F1 on 9 cities (mean ± SD): VV 0.452 ± 0.011; VH 0.383 ± 0.029; VV+VH 0.408 ± 0.031.
- **H:** under this protocol, RQ3's hypothesis (an additional polarization improves SAR change detection) is not
  supported for SAR alone: VV alone scores best and VH alone worst. This does not test Song et al.'s physical argument
  for VH on construction-ready bare land; it shows that on OSCD urban change, with 3-month mean backscatter and this
  recipe, adding VH to VV lowers F1.

## 4. Secondary results

- **DS-UNet, full input (own Holm family):** VV+VH − VV Δ +0.010, 95% CI [-0.016, +0.036], Holm p 0.41 (no detectable difference); VH − VV Δ -0.017, 95% CI [-0.039, +0.004], Holm p 0.19 (no detectable difference).
  F1: VV 0.501 ± 0.024; VH 0.484 ± 0.043; VV+VH 0.511 ± 0.019. **H:** once optical data are present, polarization does not
  measurably matter.
- **False-alarm rate by WorldCover class, SAR-only U-Net, VV+VH − VV (Holm over 7):** VV+VH lower in
  tree cover, built-up, bare, water; no detectable difference in shrubland, grassland, cropland; higher in none.
- **H (trade-off, not discrimination):** the fewer false alarms come with fewer detections. VV+VH's recall is lower than
  VV's in every class tested and overall (0.424 ± 0.081 vs 0.590 ± 0.029; precision 0.405 ± 0.040 vs 0.367 ± 0.017), so the model flags less
  change everywhere, and its F1 is lower. This is not evidence that VH separates urban development from environmental
  pseudo-change.
- **Descriptive (10 cities, Las Vegas included):** SAR-only U-Net VV+VH F1 falls to 0.224 ± 0.032 (false-alarm rate
  0.194 ± 0.051) against 0.418 ± 0.043 for VV. One city with an all-zero VH band is enough to nearly halve the pooled F1 of the
  model trained with VV+VH, so a missing polarization is itself a robustness risk.

## 5. Limitations

- One benchmark (OSCD; 9 test cities), one recipe, 3-month mean backscatter, VH range fixed at [−30, 0] dB;
  seeds are training replicates on the same cities, not independent geography.
- Thresholds are each run's validation-selected full-input threshold; the false-alarm and recall shifts depend on them.
- Minimum detectable effect 1.14 SD of the paired differences at α = 0.05/2.
